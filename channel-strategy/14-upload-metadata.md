## REUSABLE TEMPLATE (all future videos)
1. **Line 1:** restate the title's claim, sharper, ending in the open question.
2. **Line 2:** "In this video we open up X: [4 beats, comma-separated, no spoiler for the final act]."
3. **Chapters:** 8–10, named as hooks not topics ("The 0% rule" not "Reserve requirements") — chapter names show in search.
4. **Sources block:** primary sources only (regulators, central banks, government data) — the trust differentiator; never blogs.
5. **Boilerplate:** channel promise + Tuesday cadence + education disclaimer.
6. **Tags:** 12–15 — 5 exact-question phrases people search, 5 topic terms, channel name, 2–3 news-adjacent terms. Tags are the *least* important field; never spend more than 5 minutes.
7. **Pinned comment:** the most disputable fact + primary source link + one question. (Disputes = comment velocity = distribution.)
8. **End screen:** one next-video element + subscribe, final 20s, laid over the sequel-loop VO — never over the story.
