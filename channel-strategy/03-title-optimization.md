## Psychological Triggers Used

- **Contradiction of a "known fact"** (1, 3, 8, 11, 15): the title asserts something the viewer "knows" is false — the itch must be scratched. Strongest trigger class in this niche.
- **Curiosity gap with a withheld mechanism** (1, 5, 7, 17): parenthetical or second sentence promises the *how* without leaking it.
- **Personal second-person stakes** (1, 3, 12, 14, 20): "your bank," "your credit score," "your raise" — converts abstract systems into personal threat.
- **Narrative dread / storytelling** (10, 18): "How a bank dies" reads like a true-crime episode, not a lecture.
- **Specific numbers as authority** (7, 18): $1.23T and 400% do the credibility work a face normally does — critical for a faceless channel.
- **Urgency used sparingly** (4: "Yet"): finance viewers are hype-averse; manufactured urgency reads as finfluencer bait and *lowers* CTR here. Dread > urgency in this niche.

## Why the #1 Title Wins

**"Your Bank Doesn't Have Your Money (Here's What It Does Instead)"**

1. **Universal stakes:** 100% of the target audience has a bank account. No qualifier ("investors," "homeowners") shrinks the audience.
2. **True but unbelievable:** the claim survives the click — it's literally accurate (fractional reserve), so retention doesn't collapse from clickbait betrayal, and comment sections fill with "wait, is this real?" engagement.
3. **Double curiosity gap:** gap 1 — *where is my money?* gap 2 — *what does the bank do instead?* The parenthetical promises resolution, which raises CTR without revealing it.
4. **Fear without hype:** it whispers a threat rather than shouting one — on-brand for the calm-forensic positioning and algorithm-safe (no "COLLAPSE INCOMING!!" advertiser risk).
5. **Pattern-proven:** it matches the exact grammatical DNA of How Money Works' 6M-view outliers (declarative unsettling claim + parenthetical) while being a topic HMW has never packaged this way.
6. **Evergreen + spike-ready:** ranks year-round on "how do banks work" searches and gets algorithmically resurfaced during every bank scare.

**Predicted CTR band:** 6–9% in browse for a new channel (niche baseline is 3–5%), based on comparable outlier packaging in this niche.
