# Monetization Playbook — Stage Gates & Trust Rules

*Core principle: this channel's asset is trust in a niche full of grifters. Every revenue decision is filtered through one question — "would this make a skeptical viewer trust us less?" Trust compounds faster than any single sponsor check.*

## Stage Gates (unlock revenue in order; skipping stages burns trust or time)

### Stage 0 — 0 → 1K subs (months 1–3): monetize NOTHING
- No affiliate links, no Patreon, no sponsor outreach. A new channel asking for money reads as a content mill.
- Only job: packaging + retention + cadence. Revenue focus = building the high-RPM topic base (banking/credit/insurance cluster from Phase 2) so the ad machine is loaded when it switches on.

### Stage 1 — YPP eligible (~month 4–6): AdSense, tuned properly
- **Mid-roll placement is an editorial act.** Manual placement only, at act boundaries (≈3:00, 5:30, 8:00) — never inside the story or before the 1:45 stat. 3 mid-rolls on a 10:30 video is the ceiling; badly placed mid-rolls cost more retention-revenue than they earn.
- Expect $12–35 RPM on this niche (per Phase 1 research); banking/credit/insurance topics sit at the top of that band — weight the calendar accordingly in months 4–9.
- Turn OFF pre-publish ads on Shorts pages; Shorts stay discovery ads for the channel.

### Stage 2 — 10K subs / consistent 30–50K views/video: affiliates (carefully)
- **Fit test:** only products the video's *system* naturally implicates, mentioned where the script already goes. Video 1's "the one lever you hold" beat → high-yield savings account referral. Credit video → free credit-monitoring links (bureaus' own free tools first, paid tools only if genuinely better).
- **Allowed categories:** HYSA/CD platforms, budgeting apps, index-fund brokerages (majors only), credit monitoring, books.
- **Banned regardless of payout:** crypto exchanges/tokens, trading-signal apps, options/forex platforms, BNPL services (we made a video about why), "credit repair" companies, gold dealers. Any of these instantly voids the channel's positioning.
- Disclosure: verbal + description, plain words ("this link pays the channel; the free option is also in the description"). Naming the free alternative next to every paid link is the trust move competitors won't copy.

### Stage 3 — 25–50K subs: direct sponsors
- **Rate math:** finance-niche sponsor CPM runs $25–35 per 1,000 *expected* views. Channel median views × CPM = quote. (20K median views ≈ $500–700/integration; 100K ≈ $2.5–3.5K.) Never quote off a best-ever video.
- **Sponsor fit list (pursue):** budgeting/net-worth apps, HYSA banks, tax-prep software, insurance marketplaces, education platforms, password managers/VPNs (neutral fallback).
- **Integration rules:** 45–60s max · never before 3:00 (protect the hook and the 1:45 beat) · written in-voice ("the machine we just tore down is why a tool like X exists…") · one integration per video · the read is scripted by us, approved by them — never their copy verbatim.
- **Outreach kit (build at 20K subs):** one-page media sheet — niche, audience age/geo (finance advertisers pay for US/UK/CA/AU %, pull from Analytics), median views, retention average, the trust positioning ("sourced, no-hype — your brand next to credibility"). Warm targets: brands already sponsoring How Money Works / Plain Bagel tier channels — they've validated the niche spend.

### Stage 4 — 100K+ subs: owned products (the real business)
- In order of fit: **(1)** "The Machine Map" — the master schematic as a poster/digital download (the brand asset literally becomes the product) · **(2)** a paid deep-dive companion (notion/PDF "field manual" per video cluster) · **(3)** a newsletter (sponsorable, algorithm-independent — start collecting emails at 25K via a free one-page download per video) · **(4)** course ONLY if it's systems-literacy, never "make money" promises.
- YouTube membership tier: sources-and-outtakes tier only; never paywall the actual explainers.

## Revenue Mix Targets (mature channel)
| Stream | Target share | Note |
|---|---|---|
| AdSense | 35–45% | Maximized by topic mix + manual mid-rolls |
| Sponsors | 30–40% | Capped at 1/video to protect trust |
| Affiliates | 10–15% | Only category-fit, free-alternative-disclosed |
| Owned products/newsletter | 10–20% and growing | The algorithm-proof layer |

## The Trust Rules (non-negotiable, all stages)
1. Never an integration or affiliate for a product category the channel has criticized.
2. Every paid mention disclosed in plain language, verbally.
3. Free alternative named alongside every paid recommendation.
4. No sponsor touches the first 3 minutes or the story act.
5. No urgency mechanics ("first 100 people…" is fine; fake scarcity is not).
6. If a sponsor's industry blows up in the news, the channel covers it honestly or returns the money — decided in that order.
