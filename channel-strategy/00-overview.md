## Standing rules (apply to every video)
1. Thumbnail never repeats the title — together they form one curiosity gap.
2. Claim restated harder in the first 5 seconds; first visual change ≤ 4s; first pattern interrupt ≤ 15s.
3. ≥ 2 curiosity loops open at all times; spine loop closes at ~70%, ending closes *implications*.
4. Best story sits at the 40–60% retention trough.
5. Every CTA opens the next video's loop; never publish without a 1-video buffer.
6. On-screen sources for every claim — trust is the differentiator.
7. One binding constraint diagnosed and fixed per video (Phase 10 protocol); never change three variables at once.
