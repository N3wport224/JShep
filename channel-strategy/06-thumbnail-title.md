# Phase 6: Thumbnail & Title Lock-In

**Video:** "Your Bank Doesn't Have Your Money (Here's What It Does Instead)"

Rule enforced throughout: **the thumbnail never repeats the title.** The title makes the claim; the thumbnail shows the *evidence* or the *consequence*. Together they form one curiosity gap.

---

## Concept A — "The Empty Vault" *(recommended)*

- **Composition:** A massive bank-vault door swung wide open, shot from low angle. Interior: completely empty, one small paper note on the floor. Heavy vignette; single light source from inside the vault.
- **Text overlay (3 words):** **"IT'S EMPTY."** — top-left, white ultra-bold condensed, subtle red underline on "EMPTY."
- **Color psychology:** Near-black steel blues (institutional cold, secrecy) + one warm light source (revelation). Red only in the underline — alarm without ragebait.
- **Focal point:** The void inside the vault. Eyes go: bright vault opening → "IT'S EMPTY." → click. One-glance readable at 120px.
- **Pairing logic:** Title says *the bank doesn't have your money*; thumbnail shows *what that looks like*. The paper note (too small to read) adds a second micro-curiosity: "what's the note?" (Answer in-video: it's the IOU.)

## Concept B — "The IOU Slip"

- **Composition:** Extreme close-up of an ATM receipt held in a hand, but the receipt reads "IOU" in giant ledger type where the balance should be. Shallow depth of field; blurred bank branch behind.
- **Text overlay:** **"YOUR BALANCE?"** — bottom-right, yellow on black bar.
- **Color psychology:** Receipt-white against deep teal; yellow text = attention + money association without dollar-sign cliché.
- **Focal point:** The word IOU at dead center, largest element.
- **Pairing logic:** Title claims money is gone; thumbnail replaces the most familiar personal object (balance receipt) with the punchline concept. Slightly more conceptual — strong with viewers already in the niche.

## Concept C — "The Noon Line"

- **Composition:** Aerial/wide shot of an impossibly long queue of people snaking to a single small bank branch, sky ominously graded. One figure at front pulling a locked door.
- **Text overlay:** **"BY NOON."** — centered sky area, huge white with black stroke.
- **Color psychology:** Desaturated crowd, golden-hour warmth on the branch (the thing everyone wants), storm grade at frame edge (dread inbound).
- **Focal point:** The locked door where the line converges.
- **Pairing logic:** Pulls the hook's best line into the packaging; viewers who click hear "the doors close by noon" within 15 seconds — a packaging→payoff echo that boosts early retention. Risk: reads as "bank collapse news," which may attract doom-tourists who churn.

---

## Verdict

**Lock in Concept A ("IT'S EMPTY.") + the final title.**

Why this combo builds the strongest gap: the title is an abstract negation (*doesn't have*) and the thumbnail is concrete visual proof (*open vault, nothing inside*) — each strengthens the other, and neither answers the question the pair creates: *then where is it?* That answer is only available behind the click. Concept B repeats the "IOU" reveal that lands at 0:40 (burns a payoff); Concept C skews the audience toward collapse-doomers off-brand for a systems channel. A/B plan: launch with A; if CTR < 4% after 48h, test C's composition with A's text discipline.
