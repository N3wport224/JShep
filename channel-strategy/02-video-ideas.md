## Ranking Rationale — Top 5

1. **#1 Bank money (idea 1)** — biggest gap between demand (perennial searches, recent uploads multiplying) and quality of supply (mostly whiteboard/Khan-style). Universally personal ("*your* bank, *your* money"), zero prerequisite knowledge, perfectly faceless, and banks/fintech advertisers bid aggressively on this content. Evergreen with crisis-spike upside (every bank scare re-ranks it).
2. **#2 Everything expensive again** — highest raw demand right now (CPI 3.8%), but news-adjacent, so slightly less evergreen.
3. **#3 Credit score as product** — massive search volume, rising anxiety, and the "you're the product" reframe is thumbnail gold; slightly more saturated by tip-style creators (but almost nobody does the *system* view).
4. **#4 BNPL** — lowest saturation of any high-demand topic on the list; first-mover advantage available.
5. **#7 Insurance machine** — least sexy title, but virtually zero competition at explainer depth and the single best advertiser category on YouTube.

## Production & Portfolio Notes
- **Highest-RPM cluster (💰):** ideas 1, 3, 4, 7, 8, 23 — banking, credit, insurance, debt. These attract credit card, insurance, and fintech advertisers at $18–45 CPM. Weight the first 90 days toward this cluster.
- **Evergreen : trending ratio ≈ 70:30** across the list — deliberate, per solo-creator strategy.
- **All 30 are faceless-friendly:** every idea is explainable with stock footage, motion graphics, and charts; none requires interviews, location shoots, or personality-driven comedy.
- **Series seeding:** ideas 1, 5, 9, 15, 17, 28 chain into "The Machine, Part 1–6" (banks → money creation → the Fed → overnight markets → government debt → the dollar itself) — the binge architecture competitors lack.
