# Accelerated Growth Plan — 2x Cadence, Zero Flag Risk

## 1. What Actually Gets Channels Flagged (truth table)

| Myth / Fear | Reality |
|---|---|
| "Uploading too often looks like spam" | **False.** YouTube has no upload-frequency penalty. News channels upload 10+/day. More uploads = more chances at an outlier; each video is judged on its own packaging + retention. |
| "Faceless = flag risk" | Only via the **inauthentic content policy** (YPP, tightened July 2025): *mass-produced, repetitious, low-transformation* content — slideshow slop, unedited AI narration over stock loops. Our format (original scripts, custom motion graphics, sourced claims, branded structure) is exactly what that policy *protects*: it's built to separate channels like this one from the slop. |
| "AI tools get you demonetized" | AI-assisted research/scripting/editing is fine. The risk is **100% synthetic, low-effort narration presented without transformation**. Mitigation: record the VO yourself (best), or if using a synthetic voice, make it one consistent branded voice on top of heavy original production — and never fake a human identity. |
| "New channels can't upload a backlog" | Fine to upload often — but don't dump 5 videos in one hour on day 1 (that pattern *does* resemble spam import). Space uploads ≥ 1 day apart. Scheduled publishing solves this automatically. |
| Actual risk #1 | **Copyright:** unlicensed clips, network news chyrons, music. Already handled by the SOP license tracker — never skip it at higher speed. |
| Actual risk #2 | **Misleading metadata:** tag-stuffing, claims the video doesn't deliver. Our "claim survives the click" rule is the protection. |
| Actual risk #3 | **Fake engagement:** buying subs/views, sub4sub, engagement pods. Never. This is the one that kills accounts. |
| Actual risk #4 | **Repetition at scale:** if we ship faster by templating too hard (same intro lines verbatim, interchangeable scripts), we drift toward the mass-produced bucket *and* bore the audience. The skeleton stays; the hooks, stories, and metaphors must stay distinct per video. |

**Bottom line:** the ceiling on speed is our quality bar, not YouTube's tolerance.

## 2. The Accelerated Cadence

**Long-form: 2/week — Tuesday + Friday, 12:00 ET. Shorts: 5/week (Mon/Wed/Thu/Sat/Sun).**
Why not 3+/week long-form: each long-form still needs the QC bar (hook engineering, fact-check, 3 thumbnails). Below that bar, extra videos *subtract* — they train the algorithm on weak packaging and the audience on skippability. 2/week doubles at-bats while keeping every video an event. Burst to 3/week only when the buffer is ≥ 2 finished videos deep.

## 3. What 2x Costs (be honest about the machine's fuel)

Per long-form at our bar: script 6–8h (drafts exist for 6 of 8 — mostly editing/fact-check now), VO 1–2h, edit + graphics 12–16h, packaging 2h ⇒ **~2 full days/video**. At 2/week + 5 Shorts that's a 60+ hour solo week. Three ways to make it sustainable, in order of preference:

1. **Motif leverage (free):** the reusable schematic library is the whole reason 2x is possible — videos 5–8 should reuse ≥ 50% of existing graphic templates. Track "new asset minutes" per video; drive it down.
2. **Outsource the middle (first hire):** a contract editor for assembly cuts (you keep hooks, pacing pass, and final QC). $300–800/video; typically the difference between 1x and 2x for a solo creator. Hire when: 4 consecutive weeks of hitting 2x manually proves the pipeline, or the buffer dies twice.
3. **AI-assist inside the SOP (allowed, bounded):** research summaries, first-draft descriptions/tags, caption timing, rough-cut selects. Never: final scripts unreviewed, synthetic VO as a shortcut, auto-generated visuals as the primary content. The human pass is both the quality bar and the policy moat.

## 4. Throttle Rules (pre-committed, so speed never eats the channel)

- **Drop to 1/week immediately if:** any QC checklist item gets skipped to hit a date · 0:30 retention falls below 55% on two consecutive videos · the buffer hits zero twice in a month · fact-check finds an error post-publish (fix, pin correction, slow down one cycle).
- **Stay at 2/week while:** median CTR ≥ 4% · retention stable or rising · every video still gets 3 thumbnail candidates and the full fact-check.
- **Burst to 3/week only for:** a news-spike opportunity squarely in our niche (bank failure, Fed emergency move, BNPL blowup) **and** buffer ≥ 2. News-riders use the same skeleton compressed to ~8 min.
- Re-evaluate at 8 published long-forms (now ~1 month away instead of two) using the pre-registered criteria in `20-launch-checklist.md`.
