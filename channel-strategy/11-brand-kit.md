# Phase 11: Brand Kit — The Money Machine Identity System

*Purpose: every video, Short, and thumbnail should be recognizable in half a second without a face. For a faceless channel, the brand system IS the face. Build these assets once, reuse forever.*

## 1. Core Identity

- **Channel promise (one line, used everywhere):** *"We open the machines that move your money. No hype. Just the gears."*
- **Persona:** *The Calm Engineer.* Someone who has taken the machine apart, isn't angry about what they found, and is quietly showing you the gears. Never outraged, never selling, occasionally dry-funny.
- **Voice rules:** second person always ("your bank," "your money") · concede the strongest counterargument early · hedges are honesty markers ("probably," "technically," "usually") · numbers over adjectives · one metaphor per video, mechanical whenever possible (toll booth, lungs, dial, gears).
- **Banned vocabulary:** "insane," "you won't believe," "secret they don't want you to know," "hack," "get rich," "🚨," all-caps anywhere except thumbnail text.

## 2. Visual System

### Color palette
| Role | Color | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base | Machine Navy | `#0D1B2A` | Backgrounds, thumbnail base |
| Structure | Steel Blue | `#415A77` | Schematic lines, secondary graphics |
| Paper | Ledger White | `#E8E4D8` | Text, diagrams — warm off-white, never pure white |
| Signal | Brass | `#E0A458` | Money amounts, highlights, the "reveal" color |
| Alarm | Signal Red | `#C1442E` | Sparingly — one element max per thumbnail/graphic |

### Typography
- **Thumbnail/display:** Archivo Black (free, ultra-bold, reads at 120px) — max 3 words, always Ledger White or Brass on Navy.
- **In-video labels/lower-thirds:** Inter (SemiBold for labels, Regular for captions).
- **Numbers get special treatment:** always Brass, always animated (tick up/down), always sourced on screen in small Inter.

### The Schematic Motif (channel signature)
Blueprint-style line-drawn machine: gears, pipes, dials, gauges on Machine Navy, drawn-on animation (self-drawing lines, 1.5s). Appears: (1) at 0:25–0:35 of every video as the "opening the machine" beat, (2) at every act break, (3) zoomed out at the close to reveal this video's machine is one gear in a larger machine → the sequel. Each video adds its machine to a growing master schematic — by video 10 the channel has a literal connected map of the financial system (also the eventual channel trailer).

### Thumbnail grammar (every thumbnail follows this)
1. One symbolic scene, photographic, dark-graded toward Navy.
2. One light source pointing at the subject (revelation).
3. ≤ 3 words Archivo Black, top-left or centered-sky, one word may be Red or Brass.
4. Never: faces with shocked expressions, arrows, red circles, money-rain clichés, more than one text block.

## 3. Audio System
- **VO spec:** −16 LUFS integrated, close-mic, minimal room. Baseline 150 wpm; reveals ~130; story sequences ~165.
- **Music palette (3 recurring cue families, reused across videos for brand memory):** *Tick* — minimal mechanical pulse (explanation) · *Pulse* — low tension build (story/crisis) · *Warm* — resolved, human (takeaways/close). Source from one artist/pack per family so the channel "sounds like itself."
- **Signature SFX:** a soft ratchet-click for on-screen numbers, a heavy vault-thunk for act transitions, silence as the loudest tool (one full dropout per video, at the biggest stat).
- **End sound:** the machine winding down — same 2s resolve chord every video (audio logo).

## 4. Channel Meta (set once)
- **Handle:** @TheMoneyMachine (fallbacks: @MoneyMachineExplained, @TheMoneyMachineHQ)
- **Banner:** master schematic, faint, with promise line + "New machine every Tuesday."
- **About:** "The Money Machine opens up the systems that move your money — banks, the Fed, credit bureaus, insurers, the tax system — and shows you the gears. Calm, sourced, no hype. New machine every Tuesday." (front-loads keywords: money, banks, Fed, credit, explained)
- **Channel trailer:** none until 10 videos exist; then the master-schematic zoom-out cut to 45s.
- **Playlists as products:** "The Machine (in order)" · "Credit & Debt" · "Why Everything Costs More" — playlist titles are packaging too.
- **Comment policy:** pin sources comment on every video within 5 minutes of publish; reply to the first 20 substantive comments in hour 1 (velocity signal); never argue, always cite.
